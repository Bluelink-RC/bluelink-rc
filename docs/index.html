01 / Overview

Drive RC with the controller you already have.

BlueLink RC is a personal project: an on-vehicle receiver plus a companion app. An Xbox or Stadia controller is the transmitter, over Bluetooth Low Energy.

Personal / indie ESP32-S3 receiver Flutter companion

Firmware on the receiver turns stick and button input into standard RC outputs for servos and speed controllers. The companion app is for setup and status on the bench — it is not the driving radio.

On this site

This is the public documentation website for the Bluelink-RC GitHub organization. It is meant for people who want the product story — including LinkedIn and hiring readers — without access to private implementation.

Signal path

  1. 01 · Transmitter Xbox or Stadia controller

    The radio you hold. Talks to the vehicle over Bluetooth LE.

  2. 02 · Receiver On-vehicle firmware

    Binds to the gamepad and drives standard RC outputs.

  3. 03 · Vehicle Servos and ESCs

    Ordinary hobby outputs. No proprietary transmitter required.

  4. 04 · Setup Companion app

    Channel mapping, live status, and wireless firmware updates.

You drive with the controller. The app is configuration and status, not piloting.
