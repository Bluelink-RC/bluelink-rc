03 / Hardware

A small board in the vehicle.

The receiver is ESP32-S3 firmware. The current verified development board is the Seeed XIAO ESP32-S3.

Receiver

ESP32-S3. Bluetooth LE to the gamepad. PWM out to hobby servos and ESCs. Companion-app link for setup and updates.

Verified board

The Seeed XIAO ESP32-S3 is the board this project is developed and tested against. Other ESP32-S3 modules may work; they are not the reference.

Companion

Flutter app. Mobile and desktop targets exist. Used on the bench, not as the transmitter.

What you will not find here

Pin maps, wiring diagrams, bill of materials, and flashing instructions stay private. This page is only enough to know what class of hardware the project runs on.
